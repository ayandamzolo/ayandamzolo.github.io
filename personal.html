MVP 1 Challenger

A prototype for a habit I am trying to build. Spin a real complaint from everyday life, answer who, what, when, where, why and how against a clock, and earn build time for the answers that are specific rather than guessed. Then a bot uses what you shipped, as the person who complained, on a slow connection and an old phone, and only scores you if it can finish the job.

In progress. It exists because my failure mode is designing the whole platform instead of finishing version one.
